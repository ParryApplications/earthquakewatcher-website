Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EarthQuake Watcher ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our app:

  • Account information (if you choose to create an account)
  • User preferences and settings
  • Feedback and correspondence

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use EarthQuake Watcher, we may automatically collect:

  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect location data to provide earthquake information relevant to your area
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, time spent in app, crash reports
  • Analytics Data: We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our earthquake monitoring service
  • To personalize your experience with location-based earthquake data
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To send push notifications about significant earthquake events (with your consent)
  • To analyze app usage and fix technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

4.1 Google Services

  • Google Maps: For displaying earthquake locations on interactive maps
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (see Advertising section)
  • Firebase: For analytics, authentication, and cloud messaging
  • Google Play Services: For app updates and authentication

4.2 USGS (United States Geological Survey)

  • We retrieve earthquake data from USGS FDSN Web Services
  • No personal information is shared with USGS

5. Advertising and Monetization

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in our app to support free access to earthquake monitoring services. AdMob may collect and use information about you to provide personalized ads.

5.1 Information Collected for Advertising

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process the following information:

  • Device identifiers: Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID
  • IP address: For approximate location and fraud prevention
  • Location data: If you've granted location permission (approximate location only for ads)
  • Ad interaction data: Clicks, views, impressions
  • Device information: Device model, OS version, screen size
  • App usage data: Time spent in app, features used

5.2 How Advertising Data is Used

The collected advertising data is used to:

  • Serve personalized advertisements based on your interests
  • Measure ad performance and effectiveness
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Improve ad targeting and relevance
  • Generate analytics and reports for advertisers

5.3 Your Advertising Choices and Controls

For European Users (GDPR):

  • On first app launch, you'll see a consent form asking for permission to use personalized ads
  • You can choose to accept or reject personalized advertising
  • You can change your choice anytime via: Menu → More Options → Privacy Options
  • If you reject, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized based on your data

For US Users (CCPA and State Privacy Laws):

  • Personalized ads are enabled by default
  • You can opt-out anytime via: Menu → More Options → Privacy Options
  • You can opt-out of sale/sharing of personal information for advertising
  • After opting out, you'll see limited ads (non-personalized)

Device-Level Controls:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Reset your Advertising ID to limit tracking

5.4 Third-Party Advertising Partners

Google AdMob works with various advertising technology providers (ATPs) who may also collect data. These partners are bound by Google's policies and applicable privacy regulations. You can view the list of Google's advertising partners at: Google AdMob Partners

5.5 Do Not Sell My Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, sharing data with advertising partners for personalized ads may be considered a "sale" under some US state privacy laws (like CCPA). You can opt-out of this data sharing through the Privacy Options in our app.

For more information about Google's advertising practices, visit:

  • Google Ads Policies
  • How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Ads Settings

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree to share information

  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition
